Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

The short version: your projects and prices stay on your phone. We don't collect, see, store or sell any of your information. SiteSum has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and doesn't use the internet.

SiteSum – Building Calculator is a building materials calculator for Android made by MND App Development ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your settings: region, units, currency, material sizes, waste allowance and light or dark theme.
  • Your projects, the calculations you save in them, and the prices you enter.

This is kept only in the app's private storage on your device. We have no way to access it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app doesn't request internet access, so it can't send your information to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.

Suggesting your region

When you first open SiteSum, it looks at your SIM card's country, your mobile network's country and your phone's timezone to suggest your region, units and currency. This happens on your phone only. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere, and you can change the suggestion at any time.

When you share a list

If you tap Share or Share list, the app passes your materials list as text to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp or email. Nothing is shared unless you do this. Once shared, the message is handled by that app under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

SiteSum doesn't ask for any permissions.

Backups

If you've turned on Android's backup for your Google account, Android may include SiteSum's data in that backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your account; we can't access it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your information

Swipe a calculation to the left to remove it, or delete a whole project from its menu. Prices can be cleared in Settings → Price list. Uninstalling SiteSum removes all of its data from your phone. Any copy in your Android backup is managed through your Google account settings.

Children

SiteSum is a tool for builders and DIYers and isn't directed at children under 13. The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Estimates

SiteSum's results are estimates to help you plan and buy materials. Check quantities with your supplier, and structural work with your local building regulations or an engineer.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
