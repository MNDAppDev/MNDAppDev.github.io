Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026 (Mood Bubbles 1.1.0)

The short version: your moods stay on your phone. We don't collect, see, store or sell any of your information. Mood Bubbles has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and doesn't use the internet.

Mood Bubbles is a mood-tracking app for Android made by MND App Development ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The feelings you log, any shades you pick for them (like "cheeky" or "rushed"), the date and time, and any notes you add.
  • Your settings: home screen layout, light or dark theme, whether the daily reminder is on and at what time, and which share style you used last.

This is kept only in the app's private storage on your device. We have no way to access it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app doesn't request internet access, so it can't send your information to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.

When you share a mood

If you tap Save & share, or swipe an entry to share it, the app makes a picture of that one mood: the feeling, any shades and note, and when you logged it. It hands the picture and a short caption to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp. To do this it saves the picture briefly in its own private storage on your phone. Nothing is shared unless you do this, and only the mood you picked is shared. Once shared, it's handled by that app under its own privacy policy.

WhatsApp stickers

If you add a Mood Bubbles sticker pack to WhatsApp, WhatsApp reads the sticker pictures straight from the app on your phone. The stickers are just the bubble characters and their names; none of your moods, notes or settings are included. To show whether a pack is already added, the app asks WhatsApp on your phone. Nothing goes over the internet.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only used if you turn on the daily reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your phone.
  • Run at startup: lets the app restore your reminder after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: lets the reminder notification vibrate.

Backups

Backup files. If you tap Back up my moods in Settings, the app saves one file with your moods, shades, notes and settings to the place you choose, such as Google Drive or your Downloads folder. Android's own save screen does the saving; the app never sends the file anywhere itself. The file isn't encrypted, so anyone who can open it can read your moods. Keep it somewhere private. Restore from a backup reads a file you pick and adds its moods to the app.

Google backup. If backup is turned on for your Google account in your phone's settings, Android includes Mood Bubbles' moods and settings in it, so they come back when you set up a new phone or reinstall the app. Moving to a new phone with Android's "copy from your old phone" also brings them across. Google handles this under your account; we can't access it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your information

Swipe any entry to the left to delete it. Uninstalling Mood Bubbles removes all of its data from your phone. Backup files you saved are yours to keep or delete wherever you put them, and any copy in your Google backup is managed through your Google account settings.

Children

Mood Bubbles isn't directed at children under 13. The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

A note on wellbeing

Mood Bubbles is a self-reflection tool, not a medical service. If you're going through a difficult time, consider reaching out to someone you trust or a local helpline.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
